Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy covers youtube-shorts-bot, a private automation tool built and operated by Mick van der Velden. It publishes short news videos to YouTube channels owned by the operator, such as ScoreSnapDaily.

Who uses this app

The app is used only by its operator. It is not offered to the public and no other people sign in to it.

What data the app accesses

The app requests the YouTube scope youtube.upload. It uses this permission only to upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channels. It does not read, collect or store personal data from viewers or other YouTube users.

How data is stored

The OAuth refresh token is stored as an encrypted secret in a private GitHub repository. Generated videos are temporary files that are deleted after upload. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Google API Services

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time through Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on mvelden1.github.io.
